About

I like working close to the system.

A lot of my learning starts with something I use: a phone, a Linux machine, or a service in my homelab. Maintaining those systems gives me practical questions to bring back to my security studies.

I’m interested in both the hands-on work and the decisions around it: how systems are operated, how risks are assessed, and what changes when AI becomes part of the system. That’s where my interests in cybersecurity, GRC, and AI security meet.
